## Bugs and findings

1. **Medium. Order again is slow and once timed out.** `POST /v1/me/cart/reorder` took 0.9 to 3.6 s normally, 5 to 7.7 s in a burst of clicks across orders (about 1 s apart), and one request returned 504 after 10 s. Repro: on `/account/orders` press Order again on several orders in quick succession. QA ran against a remote database from a laptop, so absolute numbers are inflated, but the endpoint is far above the 150 ms read budget and should be a batched query.
2. **Low. "For business" is not in a mobile menu.** The spec says it sits in the mobile menu; below 1024 px the header link is hidden and there is no menu, so it is reachable only from the footer and the home band.
3. **Low, a11y. Focus is lost after admin revoke/restore** of a trade account (`activeElement` becomes `body`); it should land on the status line or the next button.
4. **Low, UX. Self-decision refusal is not explained.** The API returns `STAFF_SELF_CHANGE`, the admin panel shows the generic "That did not work. Try again in a moment."
5. **Low, copy. Double full stop in the order-again live region:** "Nothing was added to your cart.. 1 product was already in your cart" (Hindi the same). The visible panel text is fine.
6. **Low, admin copy. Trade "Parts bought" shows raw codes** ("tyres, lubricants") while fleet "Needs" shows labels.
7. **Low, wording. Checkout still says "business purchase (GST invoice)"** and "GST invoice for ..." (older strings; ruling R4 says "GST details on your bill" until Q97). The new pages comply.
8. **Info. The order-again panel reports added lines as a count** ("1 product added to your cart"); only missing lines are a list. The spec asks for added and missing lines as a list. The missing-line list was not exercised (every line is still sold).
9. **Info. First admin load after the staff code step showed "Something went wrong on this page."** once; a reload worked. Not reproduced.

Note: the QA API's revalidate call goes to `WEB_BASE_URL` (production), so the three home-layout saves also asked production to revalidate the `site-layout` tag. The layout was restored to the original (band shown at position 1) within minutes.

BEFORE YOU ORDER OR BOOK

Your questions, answered.

Can I buy products without booking a service?

Yes. Product shopping and the shopping bag are separate from garage bookings. Installation is optional.

Is a package price my final bill?

No. These are sample subtotals. Your vehicle, parts specifications, applicable taxes and inspection determine the workshop estimate. You approve the estimate before work.

Can the garage add work without my approval?

New or changed work requires a revised estimate. The demo billing flow uses the customer-approved items only.

Is pickup or roadside assistance confirmed?

No. Current requests are local demonstration enquiries, not dispatches. Provider coverage, timings, charges and vehicle custody terms must be confirmed before a real service.

Do all services include a warranty?

No blanket warranty is offered. Written provider terms should specify covered work, period and exclusions. Demo checklist notes are not an actual warranty contract.

How do I raise a product or repair issue?

Use Product & service support to preview a ticket linked to an order or job. Tickets stay in this browser; no live support team receives them yet.

# 06 · Workshops & service requests

Status: decided · Phase 2 · Decisions: Core flow, Workshop slots, Catalogue (reviews)

## Goal

Location-first discovery of verified workshops and a service request that moves from proposal to per-line estimate approval to bill and payment, faithful to the concept's "Know the work. Approve the cost." promise.

## User stories

- "Find a workshop": tell us where the vehicle is (GPS / saved address / pincode), which vehicle, what concern or service category, preferred date and hourly slot, pickup needed → list + map of nearby workshops (distance, verified badge, rating, services, hours, pickup) → pick one or "Let MechDost choose".
- Workshop proposes slot + expected expense range (or declines); I accept; I drop the vehicle (or pickup); workshop records odometer; sends itemised estimate; I approve line by line; work; ready; bill; pay online or at workshop; review.
- Reschedule or cancel before the vehicle is received.

## States

`requested` → (`needs_routing` if MechDost chooses) → `awaiting_workshop` → `proposed` → `confirmed` → `vehicle_received` → `estimate_sent` → `approved` (≥1 line) | `no_work_approved` → `in_progress` → `ready` → `billed` → `paid` → `completed`; `rejected` (admin), `cancelled` (customer/admin; workshop for no-show after confirmation), `rescheduled` (event, not state). A workshop **decline or acceptance timeout returns the request to `needs_routing`** for admin to reroute or reject (ruling 2026-10-31, mirrors orders); a customer may also turn down a proposal (`proposed → awaiting_workshop`). Canonical machine: `packages/shared/src/state/index.ts`.

## Data

`workshops` (concept editor fields: name, owner private, address, area, city, state, pincode, location PostGIS point, maps_url, vehicle_types[] (2W, 3W, 4W; `packages/shared/src/vehicles.ts`), ev_basic, services text, hours jsonb, pickup, location_verified, verification_method/notes/at/by, owner_consent, published, private_notes, rating, busy_mode, version), `workshop_services` (menu: title, description, inclusions, exclusions, low/high paise, duration, vehicle segments, active), `workshop_hours`, `workshop_slots` (date, hour, capacity, booked), `service_requests` (code SR-…, user, vehicle snapshot, location point + pincode + address snapshot, category, concern, preferred_at, pickup_requested, routing_mode, workshop_id, status, version, cancellable_until), `service_assignments` (as order_assignments), `service_proposals` (slot_at, scope, low/high paise, status), `estimates` (version, status) + `estimate_items` (description, qty, unit_paise, gst_rate_bp, decision pending|approved|declined), `service_events`, `service_bills` (gapless series, from approved lines, round-off), payments/refunds reused, `reviews`.

**Localised fields (hard rule 10, decision 2026-11-02 "Localised content columns").** `workshops.name_hi` and `address_hi` (business name and street transliterated: "शंकर नगर ऑटो हब"), `areas.name/name_hi` (localities: "शंकर नगर"; also used by shops and reviews), `workshop_services.title_hi`, `description_hi`, `inclusions_hi`, `exclusions_hi`; durations and hours are stored as numbers/`jsonb` and formatted per locale (never stored as English text like "Mon–Sat" or "30–60 min"). Snapshots on `service_requests` keep the text the customer saw plus the stable `service key`/`workshop_id`, so screens show the current translation by id. Missing Hindi falls back to English and is flagged in admin/partner editors. Demo mirror: `WORKSHOP_HI`, `SERVICE_HI`, `AREA_HI`, `DAY_HI` in `apps/web/src/demo/i18n/hi.ts`.

## Rules

Workshop must be `published AND location_verified` to be listed; busy mode hides from new requests; proposal slot must have capacity (conditional decrement on `workshop_slots`, exclusion constraint per workshop-hour when capacity=1); estimate revision clears prior approvals; only approved lines can be started/billed; odometer ≥ last recorded; bill = approved lines + GST per workshop registration (0% unregistered) + round-off; reviews only after `completed`, admin-approved before publish.

## Edge cases

No workshops in radius → concept "Building our Raipur workshop network" card + "Request workshop support" (admin-routed request); GPS denied → pincode centroid; customer no-show → workshop marks, admin policy; additional work found → new estimate version, customer notified; payment at workshop disputed → ticket.

## Estimates and bills (implemented in demo, 2026-11-14)

User: "add the service estimates and billing in the demo too". Browser demo (`apps/web/src/demo/store.ts`), same rules as above:

- **States** (`REQUEST_STAGES`, a subset of `serviceRequestMachine`): requested → proposed → confirmed → received (odometer) → estimate_sent → approved | no_work_approved → in_progress → ready → billed → completed. Stored as an index; records saved before 2026-11-14 (6 stages) are remapped on read (`flow: 2`).
- **Workshop job card** (`components/portal/workshop-job.tsx`): odometer (≥ the vehicle's last reading on the device) → estimate builder (lines prefilled from the requested services; description, qty 1–99, price, GST slab 0/5/12/18/28 %; up to 20 lines; per-line errors) → waiting → start approved work → ready → create bill → mark paid at the workshop; "Revise estimate" from approved, in progress or no work — not while the customer is deciding, as the machine (new version, every decision back to pending, prefilled with the approved lines); "Vehicle returned" closes a no-work job. Completed jobs are listed with the bill total.
- **Pass 4 wording (2026-11-16):** the estimate total reads "Approved total: {total}" (no "incl. GST") when no line carries GST (`approvedTotalKey`, a workshop that is not registered); the "Bill" step line follows the bill: unpaid "Pay online here, or at the workshop when you collect your vehicle.", paid online "Paid online. Thank you.", paid otherwise "Paid at the workshop. Thank you." (`billStepDetailKey`).
- **Customer** (`components/workshops/service-estimate.tsx`, `request-view.tsx`): stepper Request sent · Appointment confirmed · Vehicle received · Estimate approved · Work in progress · Ready · Bill · Paid; "Review estimate v{n}" with Approve/Decline per line (nothing preselected), live approved total, concept consent text, errors "Approve or decline every item." / "Confirm your choices using the checkbox."; a stale version is refused; the bill shows approved lines only, taxable value, CGST/SGST (rate shown when one slab), round-off and total, then "Paid at the workshop on …" and "Rate {workshop}". The account's Services link shows a badge while a proposal or estimate waits.
- **Maths** (`packages/shared/src/service-bill.ts`): tax on top per approved line (`splitExclusiveGst`, intra-state), 0 % when the workshop is not GST-registered (`workshopGstRegistered`; demo: Amapara Express Service), grand total rounded to the rupee (`invoiceRoundOff`). Property tests in `packages/shared/test/service-bill.test.ts`.
- **Analytics**: `estimate_viewed {code, version, lines}`, `job_card_approved {code, version, approved, declined}`; workshop steps as `click` ids `workshop_vehicle_received`, `workshop_estimate_sent`, `workshop_job_{start|ready|bill|paid|return}`.
- **Demo control** performs the workshop's next real action with sample values (proposal from the menu ranges, a sample estimate from the requested services, bill, paid); it never makes the customer's choices.
- **Tests**: `e2e/service-estimate.spec.ts` (per-line approval → bill from approved lines with GST and round-off → paid → rate; revision clears approvals; all declined → no work → vehicle returned; odometer and line validation; demo control; Hindi).

**Rules as built** (mirroring the demo, "Estimates and bills" above): the odometer must be ≥ the highest reading for the same customer + vehicle (saved vehicle id, or the normalised vehicle text) and the saved vehicle's odometer (422 `ODOMETER_BELOW_LAST`, `details.lastKm`); an estimate is sendable from `vehicle_received`, `approved`, `in_progress` and `no_work_approved` (never from `estimate_sent`), and each send is a new revision (the old one `superseded`, every decision pending, ≤ 20 lines checked by `estimateLineError`); start only from `approved`, ready from `in_progress`; bill only from `ready`, from the decided estimate's approved lines via `computeServiceBill` with the workshop's `gst_registered` (= has a GSTIN), frozen in `service_bills`; `mark-paid` records method and time once and moves `billed → paid → completed` (workshop, then system); `return-vehicle` closes `no_work_approved`; the `service.estimate_sent` WhatsApp is queued through the outbox when staff enabled the template (dedupe per request version). Every change writes `service_events` (append-only), `audit_log` and analytics (`service_requested`, `service_status_changed`, `service_estimate_sent`, `service_estimate_decided`, `service_billed`, `admin_workshop_saved`).

## Built: home servicing (2026-11-15, migration `home_service`)

A workshop mechanic services the vehicle at the customer's home. Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-15-home-service-design.md`; plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-15-home-service.md`; rulings: `docs/decisions.md` 2026-11-15 ("Workshop opts in", "On the final bill", "Offer to move it", "Home servicing built").

- **Mode.** `service_requests.service_mode` `workshop|home` (CHECK; home needs a pinned address snapshot, `location` and a visit fee; home never with `pickup_requested`). Same state machine, estimates, bills, reviews and notifications. "Vehicle received" is "mechanic arrived" for home jobs (step label in the customer stepper).
- **Workshop settings** (`workshops.home_service`, `home_radius_km` 1 to 25, default 5, `home_visit_fee_paise` 0 to 50 000, default 9 900; `workshop_services.home_available`). The owner edits them in the portal (`GET /workshop/home-settings`, `PUT /workshop/home-settings/{workshopId}`, permission `service_menu:write` scoped to their workshop; version CAS; audit `workshop.home_settings`); staff in the admin workshop editor (`PATCH /admin/workshops/{id}` with `homeService`, `homeRadiusKm`, `homeVisitFeePaise`, menu `homeAvailable`). The fee may not exceed the per-city setting `service.home_fee_max_paise` (spec 15; default ₹500, `FEE_ABOVE_CAP`).
- **Listing.** `GET /workshops?mode=home&lat&lng`: `w.home_service` and `ST_DWithin(w.location, point, 25 000)` (served by the partial GIST index `workshops_home_location_gist`) and `ST_DWithin(w.location, point, w.home_radius_km * 1000)` (each workshop's own radius) and at least one `home_available` active service. A PIN code alone is refused (`HOME_NEEDS_PIN`). Cards show the radius and the visit fee (GST next to it when the workshop is registered).
- **Booking.** `POST /service-requests` with `mode: "home"`, `addressId` (a live, pinned address of the customer; foreign id 404, no pin `ADDRESS_NOT_PINNED`), `visitFeePaise` (the fee the customer saw; a different current fee is `409 FEE_CHANGED`), a chosen workshop that does home visits, covers the pin and has every chosen service `home_available`. "Let MechDost choose" is not offered at home. The fee, the address text and the pin are snapshotted on the request **at booking** (the design said "at confirmed"; booking is what the customer saw, see decisions).
- **Visiting mechanic.** The workshop names the mechanic (first name) when it proposes (`mechanicName`, required for a home job); the customer sees it, the workshop's rating and the address.
- **Arrival code.** Issued when the customer accepts the proposal (`service_arrival_codes`, its own row so guesses never bump the request `version`; AES-GCM envelope like the delivery code, because the customer must read it again; the brief said "hashed", the delivery code is encrypted for the same reason). `POST /workshop/requests/{code}/vehicle-received` takes `{ odometerKm, arrivalCode, version }`: the attempt is reserved with a conditional UPDATE before any comparison, 5 wrong tries lock it, a wrong or locked answer is a `200 { ok: false, reason, attemptsLeft }` so the counter commits. Per-user rate limit `workshop_arrival_user`. `POST /admin/service-requests/{code}/arrival-override` (`service:route`, city scope; reason 5 to 300 characters and odometer required) moves `confirmed → vehicle_received` as `admin`, with the reason on the request (`arrival_override_reason/by`) and in `audit_log`. The workshop and staff views never carry the code.
- **Visit fee on the bill.** `computeServiceBill(lines, workshop, visitFee)` appends a `visit_fee` line (`kind: "visit_fee"`; GST 18 % only when the workshop is GST-registered, on top like estimate lines; a waived fee stays as a 0 line). `POST /workshop/requests/{code}/waive-visit-fee` (reason codes `goodwill|small_job|regular_customer|other`; audited; allowed until billed). Cancelled before arrival: no bill, so no fee. A declined move or no approved work: `billJob` from `no_work_approved` bills the fee alone (`service_bills.estimate_id` is nullable; a CHECK allows it only for a one-line bill); `returnVehicle` refuses with `VISIT_FEE_DUE` until the fee is billed or waived.
- **Offer to move.** Side record `service_moves` (reason `needs_lift|needs_tools|parts_unavailable|other`, transport `customer_drops|workshop_picks_up`, status `proposed|accepted|declined`, one proposed per request by partial unique index). `POST /workshop/requests/{code}/move` while `vehicle_received` or `estimate_sent`; `POST /service-requests/{code}/move { accept, version }`: accept switches `service_mode` to `workshop` (pickup follows the transport; the fee stays unless waived; timeline and audit rows, status unchanged), decline withdraws any open estimate and moves to `no_work_approved`.
- **State machine.** Two edges added, both only on the home paths: `vehicle_received → no_work_approved` by `customer` (move declined) and `confirmed → vehicle_received` also by `admin` (override). Everything else is unchanged.
- **Routing.** Admin reassign of a home request refuses a workshop that does not do home visits that far (`WORKSHOP_OUT_OF_RADIUS`); the new workshop clears the mechanic name; the customer's fee snapshot stays (the fee promised to the customer wins).
- **Web (real sign-in only; demo builds do not show the switch).** Finder and booking form: "At the workshop / At my home", pinned saved-address picker, fee line. Request page: mechanic, rating, address, fee, arrival code, move card, `visit_fee` bill line. Workshop portal: settings card, mechanic name on propose, arrival entry, waive, offer to move. Admin workshop editor: home fields. Account addresses: a lazy MapLibre pin step (tap, drag, arrow keys, "Use my location"). Strings under `homeService` (en + hi).
- **Not built:** editing the mechanic name after the proposal; email or push for the move offer and the arrival (the bell/push work lands separately; the customer sees the offer as "waiting for you" in their list); live mechanic tracking (spec 17).
- **Acceptance tests:** `packages/db/test/home-service.test.ts` (listing none/some/edge, booking refusals, code right/wrong/locked/override, odometer, bill with/without GST, waived fee, move accept/decline, foreign 404, reassign radius), `apps/api/test/home-service.test.ts` (permissions, a wrong code is a 200, override permission, public listing), `apps/web/test/home-service-views.test.ts`, state-audit rows `API-SVC-HOMEFIND`, `API-SVC-HOMEREQ`, `API-WS-HOMEJOB` and the `API-HOME-*` views.

## Built: workshops tier 1 (2026-11-15; migration `workshops_tier1`, numbered 0064 on this branch, not applied)

- **Hourly slots with capacity.** Table `workshop_slots` (workshop, IST date, hour 0 to 23, `capacity` 0 to 20, `booked`; unique per workshop-hour; CHECK `0 <= booked <= capacity`). Rows are made on demand when an hour is first booked or edited, copying `workshops.slot_capacity` (default 2, owner-editable 1 to 20); a booking is the conditional UPDATE `booked = booked + 1 where booked < capacity` (409 `SLOT_FULL`), so parallel proposals into the last place give it to exactly one. `POST /workshop/requests/{code}/propose` takes `hour` (a whole hour inside the opening hours on an open weekday, not already started: `SLOT_CLOSED`, `SLOT_PAST`); the part of the day is derived. The place is stored on the proposal (`slot_hour`, `slot_released_at`) and goes back, once, inside `transitionRequest` whenever a request moves to `awaiting_workshop`, `needs_routing`, `cancelled` or `rejected` (customer decline, cancel, reschedule, reroute, timeout). The vehicle arriving keeps it. `GET /workshop/slots?workshopId&date` (`service:read`) lists the hours with free places; `PUT /workshop/slots/{workshopId}` (`service_menu:write`) sets one hour's capacity, never below what is booked (`CAPACITY_BELOW_BOOKED`).
- **Acceptance timeout.** Setting `service.acceptance_timeout_min` (per city, 10 to 1440, default 60). The worker (every 30 s, `expireDueServiceRequests`) sends a request waiting in `awaiting_workshop` longer than that (counted from the latest event into the status, DB clock, re-read under the request lock) to `needs_routing` as actor `system`, workshop detached, event payload `{reason: 'acceptance_timeout', expiredWorkshopId, waitedMinutes}`, not shown to the customer (like a decline). Admin workshop analytics carry `expired` per workshop (index on the event payload).
- **Customer reschedule.** `POST /service-requests/{code}/reschedule {date, slot, version}` from `proposed` or `confirmed` (new transition `confirmed -> awaiting_workshop`, customer only): the live proposal is withdrawn, the held hour released, the preferred date and part of the day replaced, `reschedule_count` + 1 (at most 2: `RESCHEDULE_LIMIT`; same time: `RESCHEDULE_SAME`). The event keeps both times (`reschedule`, `fromDate`, `fromSlot`, `toDate`, `toSlot`); the request view carries `reschedule {count, max, from}` and the proposal's `hour`. The workshop answers by proposing an hour for the new time. Home visits follow the same rules (a new arrival code is issued when the customer confirms again).
- **Acceptance of a reschedule that goes unanswered** goes back to routing like any request (ruling: the old booking was already released).
- **Tests**: `packages/db/test/workshops-tier1.test.ts` (capacity race, release on cancel, decline and reschedule, closed and past hours, timeout, reschedule rules), `apps/api/test/workshops-tier1.test.ts`, `packages/shared/test/workshop-slots.test.ts`.

## Built: workshops tier 3, items 5 to 7 (2026-11-15; migration `service_bill_payments`, numbered 0068 on this branch, not applied)

- **Online payment of the bill ("Pay online").** `POST /v1/service-requests/{code}/pay` (signed-in, verified, owner only: 404 for anyone else; rate rule `service_pay_user` 20 per 10 minutes). The request must be `billed` and the bill unpaid, else 409 `BILL_NOT_PAYABLE`. The Razorpay order amount is the bill's `total_paise` read in the database (home visit fee and GST already on the bill; the client sends nothing); one open order per bill (`payments_one_open_per_bill_uq`), so a double tap or a retry returns the same order. The checkout callback `POST /v1/payments/razorpay/verify` and the webhook are the order flow's, extended by purpose. A capture settles the bill (`method = online`, `paid_at`) and moves the request `billed -> paid -> completed` by the system in one transaction (the booking coins reward follows `paid` as before). Browser: `apps/web/src/lib/payments/pay-bill.ts`, loaded only on the tap (dynamic import; Razorpay checkout.js as the order flow).
- **Online and "paid at the workshop" never both settle.** Both take the request row first (lock order request -> bill -> payment) and the loser finds the request no longer `billed`: the workshop's mark is a 409 `WRONG_STEP`; a capture that arrives after the workshop marked it paid (or after a staff cancel) is recorded on its payment row, audited `payment.captured_needs_refund` once, and ONE refund request is raised in the refunds queue (source `duplicate_payment`, or `service_cancelled` after a cancel; idempotent per payment). The bill is never settled twice.
- **No coupons or coins on service bills.** Specs 03 and 20 apply them to product orders only; the bill is a snapshot of approved lines (builder ruling, below). Workshop analytics counts cash, UPI and card; online-paid bills show in billed totals but not in a payment-method column (not built).
- **Staff cancel.** Permission `service:cancel` (new, in ops_manager, city_manager and super_admin; needs `pnpm db:seed` to sync the catalogue), `POST /v1/admin/service-requests/{code}/cancel { reasonCode, version }`, city-scoped like routing (foreign city 404). Reason codes (`SERVICE_STAFF_CANCEL_REASONS`): workshop_unavailable, workshop_unresponsive, customer_asked, duplicate_booking, policy_violation, other. Allowed from every live state; the machine gained admin-only `cancelled` edges from `vehicle_received`, `estimate_sent`, `approved`, `no_work_approved`, `in_progress`, `ready`, `billed` and `paid` (customer and workshop edges unchanged; `completed` stays final: a finished job is a support ticket and the refunds queue, spec 05). The held hour goes back exactly once (`releaseRequestSlots`), pending proposals are withdrawn, a paid bill gets ONE refund request (approval-first, finance approves), the customer gets the new email `service_cancelled` (en + hi), the bell notice `service.cancelled` and push, and the event, audit row and analytics event `service_cancelled_by_staff` are written in the same transaction. A paid bill is normally `completed` at once, so in practice the refund path serves the transient `paid` state and late captures; see Q151.
- **Ranked suggestions.** `GET /v1/admin/service-requests/{code}/suggestions` (`service:route`; only for `needs_routing`, `awaiting_workshop`, `proposed`): eligible workshops (same rules as reassign) ranked by the pure `rankWorkshops` (`packages/shared/src/workshop-ranking.ts`): distance (PostGIS, inside 25 km of the pin; no pin = neutral), rating, acceptance (proposed / proposed + declined + timed out, last 90 days), median minutes to propose, load (open jobs / (capacity x 4)). Integer basis points, equal weights, busy / paused / suspended never ranked, ties by score then distance then id. Two SQL statements however many workshops. The admin Services card shows the list with the reasons in words and "Best match"; one click routes (the existing reassign); a failed ranking falls back to the nearest-PIN list.
- **Tests**: `packages/db/test/service-bill-payments.test.ts` (17), `apps/api/test/service-bill-payments.test.ts` (9), `packages/shared/test/workshop-ranking.test.ts` (7, seeded property runs; fast-check is not a dependency), machine edges in `state.test.ts`.
- **Not built**: customer "confirm" of a workshop-recorded cash payment (the workshop marks, as before), online column in workshop analytics, admin-tunable ranking weights, a staff screen listing stuck Razorpay orders for bills, expiry of abandoned bill orders (they stay open and cost nothing; a capture on one is refunded).

## Advisor brief (key lines)

- **Context loaded:** CLAUDE.md, progress, decisions "2026-11-15 Next tier answers", spec 08/13, design, RBAC catalogue, rbac repo, grant CLI, customer blocks, auth middleware, session cache.
- **Findings that change the design:** (1) `customers:read_pii` already exists (the design says `customers:pii`): reuse it, no new permission for it. (2) `audit:read` and `staff:manage` exist: no permission is added, so no `db:seed` is needed at merge. (3) No "last sign-in" is stored anywhere: staff sign-in success is written to `audit_log` as `auth.staff_signed_in` (one row per staff sign-in), and the screen reads the latest. (4) 2FA here is "password plus a fresh phone code", not TOTP: the state shown is `ready` (phone verified) or `phone_missing` (that staff member cannot complete step two until a phone is verified). (5) Customers have no home city, so all customer routes stay all-cities-only (existing `globalOnly`).
- **Risks:** (a) two super admins revoking each other at once leaves none: an advisory lock plus a "last super admin" check inside the revoke transaction. (b) the audit viewer shows `before/after` JSON that can hold contact details: the viewer always masks them, for everyone (customer view is the one audited unmasking path). (c) search is an unbounded scan without indexes: expression indexes on `right(phone,10)` and `lower(name) text_pattern_ops` (migration), codes and email use existing unique indexes; audit filters get `(action,id)`, `(entity,id)`, `(actor_user_id,id)`, `(created_at)`.
- **Applicable learnings:** L100 (own locale namespaces, text insertion), L101/L102 (db repos never import runtime values from shared DTO modules), L145 (real-mode views in the state-audit model), T-series (role-leak matrix on every route).
- **Skills:** mechdost-advisor, writing-plans, test-driven-development, firebase-phone-auth-rbac, domain-modeling, drizzle-postgis-supabase, supabase-postgres-best-practices, hono, vercel-react-best-practices, ui-ux-pro-max, shadcn-component-discovery, frontend-design, shadcn-component-review, web-design-guidelines, accessibility, next-intl-hindi, website-testing, verification-before-completion.

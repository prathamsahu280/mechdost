## Rulings (cost if wrong)

- Staff invites are bound to the phone (no token): the invitee's OTP-verified phone is the proof, as for seller
  applicants (`linkApplicantOwner`). They are claimed in the same sign-in hook. Cost if wrong: an invite sent to a
  wrong number lets that person in until the owner cancels it; the owner sees pending invites and can cancel.
- The invite reuses `partners.invite_days` (no new setting: new settings need labels outside this namespace). Cost: one
  knob for both invite kinds.
- Max 10 people per shop = owner + staff + pending invites, a constant (`SHOP_TEAM_MAX`), checked under the shop row lock.
- Busy toggle is owner-only (like the hours), no version check (a toggle is idempotent); it bumps the shop `version`, so
  an hours edit open at that moment gets the existing STALE_SHOP reload.
- Staff do not see money: `payoutPaise` and `unitPaise` come back null for shops where the caller lacks
  `payouts:read` (cash to collect stays: the person at the door needs it).
- Delivery-code lock is derived from `shipments.delivery_code_attempts` (already server state): the list says
  `deliveryLocked`; no new column.
- Late accept: `acceptAssignment` explains a failed compare-and-set by reading the row: past its time → `OFFER_EXPIRED`.

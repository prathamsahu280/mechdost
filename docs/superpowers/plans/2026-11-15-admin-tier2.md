# Admin next tier, part 2: implementation plan

Date: 2026-11-15 · Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-15-admin-next-tier-design.md` items 5 to 8 · Specs 08, 12, 14, 15
(item 4, CSV exports, waits for the storage pipeline and is not in this plan).

Advisor brief (mechdost-advisor, 2026-11-15): conflicts none; risks (1) JS budget: the footer and home page are in the
header/layout import graph, so every new piece there is a server-only module; (2) data integrity: history must be
append-only and numbered without races; (3) cache: the website must refresh by tag after a publish or the editor looks
broken; mitigations in the tasks below. Applicable learnings: L174 (targeted checks only), L100 (own locale namespace),
T101/T102 (role-leak matrix, 20-character tokens).

## Tasks (test first where there is logic)

1. **Shared pure modules** with tests: `company.ts` (fields, formats, `companyFor`, email line), `site-layout.ts`
   (lists, strict clean, defaults); policy tokens unchanged. Exports in `package.json`.
2. **Schema + migration** `content_versions` (append-only trigger, RLS, backfill of published rows as version 1);
   drizzle-kit generate, renamed `0061_admin_policy_history`.
3. **Repositories** (`content.ts` history + restore with advisory lock; `site-config.ts` layout and company
   documents with compare-and-set, audit, events; `settings-admin.ts` city events and `listCitiesInScope`). DB tests.
4. **API**: history and restore routes; `routes/site-config.ts` (public layout and company, admin layout and company);
   `GET /admin/settings/cities`; `revalidate.ts` (secret, tags, best effort); email adapter reads the grievance line from
   the company details with the env value as fallback. API tests with a role-leak matrix.
5. **Website server side** (no client code): `/api/revalidate` route and its pure check; `site-config-content.ts`
   (cached reads by tag); footer legal block and copyright as async server children; policy pages show the published
   text and the grievance officer card; company page shows the CIN; home page applies the layout.
6. **Admin screens** (real sign-in only, lazy): policy editor, layout editor, city picker + city values, company form.
   ui-ux-pro-max / shadcn discovery: the screens reuse the portal's own form pieces (no registry component fits a
   section list editor); a11y: labelled controls, 44 px targets, inline confirm instead of a modal.
7. **Locale strings** `adminTier2` and `siteCompany` in en and hi; analytics events; state-audit rows and fixtures;
   checklist regenerated.
8. **Docs and verification**: specs 08, 12, 14, 15, decisions, progress, deploy runbook, learnings; targeted tests,
   typecheck and ESLint/Prettier on touched files; no full gate, no Playwright, no build.

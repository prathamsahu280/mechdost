# MechDost delivery agents: market, legal and risk research

Prepared 2026-11-26 for the delivery-agent feature (project question Q217, `docs/questions-for-user.md`). This is research only. It is not legal or tax advice. Every legal and tax point below should be confirmed by a Chhattisgarh labour lawyer and a CA before launch.

- **[P]** primary source: a statute, gazette, government site or the company's own page.
- **[S]** secondary source: a law-firm note, news report or trade press.
- **[V]** vendor or competitor marketing, or a job-listing site. Treat it as indicative only.
- **[U]** unverified: I could not confirm it, or the sources conflict.

## 0. Key findings, and what I could not verify

### Top findings

1. **The central gig-worker regime is live.** The Code on Social Security 2020 took effect on 21 Nov 2025 [S]. The Social Security (Central) Rules 2026 were notified on 8 May 2026 [S]. Aggregators had to onboard to eShram, with an API integration, by 21 Jun 2026 under a Ministry of Labour circular of 1 Jun 2026 [S]. The aggregator contribution is 1–2% of annual turnover, capped at 5% of payouts to gig workers [P, PIB]. **Its start date has to be notified separately, and I found no such notification [U].**
2. **Chhattisgarh has no state gig-worker act that I could find.** Rajasthan (2023), Karnataka (2025, a 1% fee collected from Feb 2026), Jharkhand (2025) and Telangana (2026) have one. Chhattisgarh's assembly debated gig workers' safety in Feb 2026 [S], and roughly 2 lakh workers in the state can now register on eShram [S]. Even so, design to the strictest state rules now (written reasons and notice before deactivation, published allocation and pay rules, grievance handling). They are cheap to build and portable when MechDost moves to other states.
3. **Vehicles are the hardest legal constraint, not labour law.** A white-plate (private) bike used for paid delivery is a grey area. The MV Aggregator Guidelines 2025 let a **state** authorise private bikes through aggregators [S], but I found no Chhattisgarh adoption [U]. Private-use motor insurance can refuse claims for commercial use [S]. Truck batteries (up to 60 kg), truck tyres and barrels physically need a three-wheeler or a small goods vehicle. That means a transport-registered (yellow-plate) vehicle with commercial insurance. Porter's own two-wheeler tier is capped at **20 kg** [P, Porter Jaipur page].
4. **Lead-acid batteries are dangerous goods everywhere I could check, but the Indian rule for carrying them on a two-wheeler is unverified.** CMVR Rule 129 covers "goods carriages" carrying dangerous goods [S]. ADR special provision 598 exempts secured new batteries in Europe [S]. Many foreign couriers ban wet car batteries [S]. The safe policy is to carry upright, sealed, new batteries only, in a fitted crate or rack, with acid-spill kits, on the right vehicle tier. Old-battery pickup ("exchange") is hazardous waste under the Battery Waste Management Rules 2022 [S] and should go through the shop or brand's take-back route, not an agent's bike.
5. **GST on delivery has changed twice.** Notification 17/2025-CT(R), effective 22 Sep 2025, made the e-commerce operator (ECO) pay 18% on local delivery supplied through it by unregistered delivery persons [S, EY]. Notifications 06, 07 and 09/2026-CT(R) of 27 Oct 2026 cut "specified" delivery services through an ECO to **5% without ITC from 1 Nov 2026** [S, TaxGuru]. Whether MechDost's model is "local delivery through an ECO" or MechDost's own supply of delivery bundled with the goods is a question for the CA.
6. **TDS: payouts to agents are contract payments.** Section 194C (1% for individuals, ₹30,000 single or ₹1,00,000 a year threshold, 20% with no PAN) [S] now maps to s.393 of the Income-tax Act 2025 from 1 Apr 2026 [S]. PAN is therefore effectively mandatory at onboarding.
7. **Don't collect Aadhaar.** Use DL plus PAN, with the DL checked through DigiLocker or an RC/DL API. Aadhaar adds vault and handling rules [S], and UIDAI is moving to ban photocopies [S/U]. If Aadhaar is ever needed, use UIDAI offline verification (signed XML or QR, keep the last 4 digits only).
8. **Customer safety is the reputational risk.** There are documented cases of riders harassing women customers after getting their number from the app [S, 2026]. Masked calling, revealing the address only once the parcel is picked up, and cutting contact off after delivery are the minimum.
9. **COD is the biggest fraud exposure.** Industry practice is a cash-in-hand cap (about 10–15 orders' worth), daily deposit, and a ban for skipping deposit [S/V; Borzo states a permanent ban, P].
10. **Pay in tier-2 cities is low and disputed.** Food and q-commerce riders get about ₹15–35 base per order, ₹50–70 with incentives [S/V], and earn ₹10–15k a month in tier-2 cities [V]. Heavy-goods delivery is different work: loading, stairs, a bigger vehicle. Pay for it should be set per vehicle tier, not copied from food delivery.

### 1.2 Onboarding documents (industry norm, adapted)

Common set [V, registrationwala](https://www.registrationwala.com/knowledge-base/post/learning/how-to-become-swiggy-delivery-partner); [P, Shadowfax](https://www.shadowfax.in/riders-page):

- Driving licence with the right class (MCWG for a motorcycle; transport endorsement for a 3W or goods vehicle).
- Vehicle RC, in the agent's name or with the owner's authorisation letter.
- Vehicle insurance: commercial or goods-carrying for 3W/4W; see 2.5 for 2W.
- PAN (needed for TDS; 20% TDS without it [S](https://www.teachoo.com/949/367/Section-194C-TDS-on-Contractors/category/Rates-of-TDS/)).
- Bank account (cancelled cheque or penny-drop verification).
- Selfie, with a liveness check, to stop impersonation.
- Emergency contact.
- Pollution certificate and, for transport vehicles, fitness and permit.

**Recommended for MechDost [E]:** DL plus RC plus PAN plus bank, verified through APIs; no Aadhaar stored (2.7). Police verification is required of ride-hailing drivers under MVAG 2025 [S, Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india%2Fexplained-safety-security-welfare-what-the-new-ride-hailing-rules-mean-3614987). It is not proven to be required for delivery, but it is the norm. Bengaluru police are drafting onboarding SOPs for q-commerce riders [S, Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india%2Fkarnataka%2Fbengaluru%2Fquick-commerce-delivery-agents-face-scrutiny-as-police-plan-safeguards-3783469). Chhattisgarh Police appears to run a citizen portal for character certificates and employee verification at citizenportal.cgpolice.gov.in [S/U, third-party listing](https://complainthub.org/chhattisgarh-police-help/).

### 1.3a Training

- Shadowfax makes a paid training order compulsory before the first live order, paid at ₹5/km to the onboarding centre [P, Shadowfax](https://www.shadowfax.in/riders-page).
- Amazon Flex gives every partner a background check plus hands-on training before starting [P, aboutamazon.in](https://www.aboutamazon.in/news/operations/heres-why-amazon-flex-is-a-great-choice-for-delivery-associates).
- MVAG 2025 (ride-hailing) sets a 40-hour induction plus refresher training for low-rated drivers [S, Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india%2Fexplained-safety-security-welfare-what-the-new-ride-hailing-rules-mean-3614987).
- **For MechDost [E]:** a 1–2 hour module (in Hindi) on the app, the delivery code, privacy rules, COD and **safe handling of batteries and tyres** (lifting, upright carriage, spill response), then one supervised trial delivery. Record completion before the first offer.

### 1.4 App flow (general industry practice; internals of named platforms not documented [U])

1. **Online/offline toggle.** Only online agents get offers. Insurance at the big platforms typically applies only while logged in or on a trip [S, gocredit](https://gocredit.money/news/gig-worker-insurance-what-0-cover-means-for-you-20261027).
2. **Offer with an accept timer.** The project's own research cites about a 30-second accept window, then the offer passes to the next-closest rider [S/U, `docs/research/industry-features.md` citing raftlabs].
3. **Navigate to pickup.** Arrival is detected by geofence. The shop hands over and either the shop or the agent confirms with a **pickup code or OTP plus a photo** of the sealed parcel.
4. **Exact customer address and call button unlock after pickup** (recommended, 3.2).
5. **Deliver.** The customer gives the **delivery code** (MechDost already has a 4-digit code). The agent takes a proof photo and collects COD (cash or UPI QR).
6. **Deposit COD.** The app shows cash-in-hand, and accepting new orders is blocked above the cap.
7. **Earnings screen and weekly payout.** Shadowfax: weekly withdrawal [P](https://shadowfax.in/blogs/shadowfax-courier-earnings-guide). Swiggy riders: weekly [S, project research]. Borzo: daily on weekdays [P].
8. **Support/SOS.** Swiggy's "Delivering Safely" charter includes an on-demand ambulance [S, Zee Biz](https://www.zeebiz.com/companies/news-swiggy-launches-delivering-safely-charter-for-delivery-partner-safety-accidental-medical-coverage-on-demand-ambulance-other-features-of-program-280648).

### 1.5 Assignment and dispatch models

| Model                                  | How it works                                               | Fit for MechDost                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Admin-assigned**                     | Staff pick the agent                                       | **Phase 1.** Matches how MechDost already routes orders to shops. Volume is low and items are heavy, so a human check of vehicle tier and weight is valuable.                                            |
| **Nearest-idle auto-offer with timer** | Offer the nearest eligible agent; on timeout, the next one | Phase 2, once there are more than about 30 deliveries a day                                                                                                                                              |
| **Broadcast** (first to accept wins)   | Everyone in range sees it                                  | Simple but encourages fights and cherry-picking; avoid it, or use it only as a fallback                                                                                                                  |
| **Batching**                           | One run, several drops                                     | Industry norm in q-commerce [S, Mint](https://www.magzter.com/en/stories/newspaper/Mint-New-Delhi/QUICK-COMMERCE-USES-ALGOS-TO-BUNCH-ORDERS-SAVE-COSTS); useful later for small parts from the same shop |

The constraint every model must respect is **vehicle tier ≥ order's heaviest or bulkiest item** [E]. Telangana's 2026 Act requires aggregators to **publish their allocation, pay-calculation and rating default settings** (s.14) [S, Bar & Bench](https://www.barandbench.com/columns/beyond-the-app-why-telanganas-new-law-is-a-major-step-for-gig-worker-dignity). Write the dispatch rules down now.

### 1.6 Pay models

Components seen across the industry:

- **Base per order**, by distance. Food base about ₹20–25, down from about ₹40 a few years earlier [S, india.com](https://www.india.com/business/zomato-swiggy-zepto-delivery-boys-earn-this-much-on-every-order-incentives-include-if-they-meet-an-accident-then-7582449/).
- **Per-km beyond the base.** Rapido captains about ₹6–10/km [V].
- **Waiting time.** Porter bills the customer after 25 minutes [P, Porter]. No rider-side figure found [U].
- **Incentives and surge.** About ₹10–20 per order in rain or peak [V, PickMyWork](https://www.pickmywork.com/blinkit-zepto-swiggy-delivery-partner-earnings-2026/); ₹120–150 per order at the New Year's Eve 2025 peak [S, LatestLY](https://www.latestly.com/business/swiggy-zomato-hike-delivery-incentives-for-new-years-eve-amid-nationwide-gig-workers-strike-call-heres-everything-you-need-to-know-7258063.html). Incentives tied to "never cancel" are a known complaint [S].
- **Minimum guarantee and joining bonus** (Shadowfax) [P].
- **Tips.** Only 3–5% of orders get a tip [S, Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/how-it-works/only-3-5-of-indian-delivery-orders-receive-tips-report-87226.htm).
- **Weekly payout** is the norm. Daily or instant payout is a differentiator.

Reported earnings: food riders ₹50–70 per order including incentives, ₹100–120 at peak, while base pay in some cases fell to ₹15–20 [S, Storyboard18 via search summary]. Tier-2 monthly earnings ₹10–15k (Blinkit), ₹10–14k (Zepto) [V, PickMyWork]. Zomato average ₹28k a month per its CEO (Feb 2025) [S, Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/technology/news/story/rs-28000-average-monthly-earnings-deepinder-goyal-shares-how-zomato-revolutionised-gig-workers-earnings-and-benefits-463251-2025-02-03).

### 1.7 Ratings, penalties, deactivation, appeals

- Zomato reportedly removes about 5,000 delivery partners a month for violations (CEO statement, undated) [S, Lokmat](https://www.lokmattimes.com/technology/zomato-terminates-5000-delivery-boys-every-month-says-ceo-deepinder-goyal-a517).
- Typical stated reasons: suspected fraud, customer complaints, low acceptance or ratings, fake GPS, expired documents. Appeals go through an in-app form ("Swiggy Connect", 24–48 h response) and then a grievance officer [S, community guide](https://righttoinformation.wiki/practical-guides/food-delivery-partner-account-deactivated-payout-withheld); [S, consumercomplaints](https://www.consumercomplaints.in/swiggy-swiggy-delivery-id-deactivated-without-any-reason-c3156942).
- **What the law now asks for:** Karnataka requires reasons in the contract, **14 days' notice** before termination, an internal dispute committee (14 days, then the Board), disclosure of automated decision-making, and a stated right to refuse tasks [P/S, PRS](https://prsindia.org/bills/states/the-karnataka-platform-based-gig-workers-social-security-and-welfare-bill-2025). Telangana requires a **written reason plus 7 days' notice** before deactivation, except for "imminent physical danger" [S, Bar & Bench]. In Raipur a blocked worker "can file a complaint and has a right to a hearing" [S, Patrika, Apr 2026](https://www.patrika.com/raipur-news/major-relief-for-200-000-gig-workers-in-chhattisgarh-now-entitled-to-register-20496354).
- MVAG 2025 (ride-hailing) requires refresher training for drivers in the bottom ~5% of ratings [S, sources disagree on frequency](https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india%2Fexplained-safety-security-welfare-what-the-new-ride-hailing-rules-mean-3614987).

**Recommended [E]:** an immediate _suspension_ only for safety or fraud, with a reason code. Any other _deactivation_ gets a written reason plus 7 days' notice plus an appeal to a second staff member. All of it is audited, reusing MechDost's existing "pause/suspend with reason, history kept" pattern.

## 2. Law and compliance

### 2.1 Code on Social Security 2020 (central)

- **In force from 21 Nov 2025** [S, LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/articles/gig-platform-workers-code-on-social-security2020-543928); [P, PIB backgrounder](https://pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2200767).
- **"Aggregator"** (s.2(2)): a digital intermediary or marketplace connecting buyers or users with sellers or service providers. Contribution duties (s.114(4)) apply only to Seventh Schedule categories. Reported lists include ride sharing, food and grocery delivery, **logistics**, marketplace and inventory **e-commerce**, professional services, and a residual "any other goods and services provider platform"; secondary sources disagree on the exact list [S, Taxmann](https://www.taxmann.com/post/blog/analysis-aggregator-obligations-code-on-social-security/); [S, PSA](https://psalegal.com/code-on-social-security-impact-on-online-aggregators/). **MechDost with its own agents is very likely in scope [E].**
- **Contribution:** 1–2% of annual turnover, capped at 5% of amounts paid or payable to gig and platform workers [P, PIB](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/dec/doc2025129723601.pdf); [S, Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/news/gig-workers-secured-under-new-labour-codes-as-aggregators-must-pay-2-of-turnover). The commencement date is to be notified, and **I found no notification** [U, Chambers](https://chambers.com/articles/epf-compliance-in-the-gig-economy-is-indias-new-social-security-framework-enough).
- **Social Security (Central) Rules 2026**, notified 8 May 2026: self-assessment, provisional contribution and final returns; registration and upload of workers within 45 days [S, DLA Piper](https://knowledge.dlapiper.com/dlapiperknowledge/globalemploymentlatestdevelopments/2026/india-mandates-aggregator-onboarding-to-eshram-portal-to-expand-social-security-coverage); [S, Digital Policy Alert](https://digitalpolicyalert.org/event/39808-social-security-central-rules-2026-including-worker-protection-regulation-entered-into-force).
- **eShram onboarding plus API integration by 21 Jun 2026** (MoLE circular of 1 Jun 2026); penalties under s.133 [S, DLA Piper].
- **90-day engagement threshold** for worker benefits was in the draft rules; final status not confirmed [U, The Quint](https://www.thequint.com/news/breaking-news/centre-sets-90-day-rule-gig-workers-strike-social-security-benefits-latest).
- **Action [E]:** when the first agent is onboarded, register MechDost as an aggregator on eShram and report agents through it. **Budget up to 5% of agent payouts.** For a parts marketplace, 1–2% of turnover will far exceed agent payouts, so the 5%-of-payouts cap is what binds. A lawyer should confirm the turnover base and whether goods turnover counts.

### 2.3 Employee vs independent contractor

- The pending Supreme Court case _IFAT v Union of India_ (W.P.(C) 1068/2021) argues that platforms misclassify workers. The latest order seen was dated 9 Apr 2026, listed again for 7 May 2026; outcome not found [P, SCI order](https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2021/22576/22576_2021_8_20_69898_Order_09-Apr-2026.pdf); [S, SC Observer](https://www.scobserver.in/cases/gig-workers-access-to-social-security-the-indian-federation-of-app-based-transport-workers-ifat-v-union-of-india/).
- The Karnataka HC (Sep 2024) treated an Ola driver as an employee _for PoSH purposes_; Ola has appealed [S, L&E Global](https://leglobal.law/2024/10/29/india-gig-workers-engaged-by-taxi-aggregators-can-be-considered-employees-under-indian-sexual-harassment-law/).
- PF authorities look at substance over form: control over how, when and where work is done, and economic dependence [S, Corrida Legal](https://corridalegal.com/the-code-on-social-security-2020-epf-gig-worker-registration-explanation/). ESI generally applies at 10+ employees and EPF at 20+, and coverage stays once triggered [S, Treelife](https://treelife.in/compliance/esi-compliance-in-india/). **With 10–30 agents assigned by admin, fixed shifts and uniforms, MechDost is near those thresholds if a court treats the agents as employees [E].**
- **Contractor-friendly design [E]:** agents choose when to go online. They can decline an offer without penalty (Karnataka requires a stated right to refuse). They own their vehicle. Pay is per task. There is no exclusivity clause, no fixed shifts in the contract, and a written partner agreement. Avoid minimum-hours rules. Optional minimum-earnings guarantees are fine if framed as an incentive.
- An alternative for heavy goods is to contract a **fleet or 3PL** (Porter Enterprise, Shiprocket Quick, Borzo), which moves the labour and vehicle risk to them.

### 2.4 Tax

- **TDS on agent payouts:** 194C (contractor), 1% to individuals, threshold ₹30,000 single or ₹1,00,000 a year, **20% with no PAN** [S, Teachoo](https://www.teachoo.com/949/367/Section-194C-TDS-on-Contractors/category/Rates-of-TDS/); [P, Income Tax Dept](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/section-194c). From 1 Apr 2026, the Income-tax Act 2025 s.393(1) applies, rates carried over [S, Busy](https://busy.in/tds/section-194c-contractors-subcontractors.md). A part-time agent may stay under ₹1 lakh a year; a full-time one will cross it in about 4–8 months [E]. 194H (commission) does not fit; 194-O (0.1%) applies to MechDost's _sellers_, not agents [S, Income Tax Dept](https://incometaxindia.gov.in/w/section-194-o-6).
- **GST on delivery:**
  - From 22 Sep 2025, Notification 17/2025-CT(R) made the ECO liable under s.9(5) for local delivery supplied through it by persons not required to register, at **18%** [S, EY](https://www.ey.com/en_in/technical/alerts-hub/2025/09/cbic-issues-notifications-giving-effect-to-the-recommendations-made); [S, CA Club](https://www.caclubindia.com/notice_circulars/cgst-rate-notification-17-2025-local-delivery-services-effective-22-09-2025--11491.asp). Notification 16/2025 removes such delivery from the GTA definition [S, TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/?p=990007).
  - The 57th GST Council (8 Oct 2026) recommended **5% without ITC** [S, Taxscan](https://www.taxscan.in/gst/gst-council-recommends-5-gst-without-itc-on-delivery-services-through-e-commerce-platforms). TaxGuru reports Notifications 06, 07 and 09/2026-CT(R) of 27 Oct 2026, **effective 1 Nov 2026** [S, TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/?p=1101564). Scope ("specified" delivery services) not read in the gazette [U].
  - **Open question for the CA:** is MechDost (a) an ECO through which agents supply delivery to the customer (s.9(5)), or (b) the supplier of delivery itself, buying services from unregistered agents? This changes invoicing, the delivery-fee line and the invoice-number series. Agents themselves are almost always below the registration threshold.

### 2.5 Motor Vehicles Act: vehicles, permits, insurance

- **White-plate bikes for paid delivery:** MoRTH's Motor Vehicle Aggregator Guidelines 2025 (1 Jul 2025) let states permit non-transport motorcycles through aggregators under s.67(3) MV Act; it is a state option, possibly with a fee [S, ThePrint](https://theprint.in/economy/centre-allows-use-of-non-transport-motorcycles-for-passenger-journeys-through-aggregators/2676706); [S, Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/india/centre-clears-way-for-private-two-wheelers-as-bike-taxis-under-mv-aggregator-guidelines-2025-states-to-decide-on-rollout). Only Rajasthan had fully adopted it as of Gadkari's remarks [S, Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/nitin-gadkari-urges-states-to-adopt-aggregator-guidelines-amid-bike-taxi-crackdowns/). Chhattisgarh adoption was not found, and whether the guidelines cover goods delivery is unverified [U]. In practice the food and q-commerce industry runs on white-plate bikes, and enforcement has been sporadic. Delhi's 2023 bike-taxi ban hit Swiggy and Zomato riders [S, Business Standard](https://business-standard.com/article/current-affairs/delhi-bike-taxi-ban-leaves-swiggy-zomato-delivery-execs-stuck-in-crossfire-123030101023_1.html).
- **MVAG 2025 duties (ride-hailing; applicability to delivery unverified):** driver health cover ₹5 lakh, term cover ₹10 lakh, police verification, medical and psychological checks, 40-hour induction training [S, Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india%2Fexplained-safety-security-welfare-what-the-new-ride-hailing-rules-mean-3614987); [S, InsightsIAS](https://www.insightsonindia.com/2025/07/04/motor-vehicle-aggregator-guidelines-mvag-2025/). Useful as a **benchmark**, not a proven obligation.
- **Insurance:** a private-use bike policy may reject an own-damage claim if the bike was used for deliveries without telling the insurer; add-ons fall with the base claim [S, Coverfox](https://www.coverfox.com/two-wheeler-insurance/articles/common-reasons-your-bike-insurance-claim-can-be-rejected); [P, Universal Sompo wording](https://www.universalsompo.com/assets/file/motor-two-wheeler-bundled/motor-two-wheeler-bundled-addons.pdf). Third-party liability is still compulsory. **Mitigations [E]:** require agents to declare delivery use to their insurer or hold a commercial 2W policy, and buy MechDost's own group personal-accident cover for agents (2.10).
- **Low-speed e-scooters** (≤250 W, ≤25 km/h) are exempt from registration and DL [S, Bajaj General](https://www.bajajgeneralinsurance.com/blog/motor-insurance-articles/is-it-legal-to-ride-electric-scooter-without-registration-in-india.html). The specs are under review (June 2026) [S, Autocar](https://www.autocarindia.com/industry/power-and-weight-limits-for-low-speed-electric-two-wheelers-under-scrutiny-439907). **Not suitable** for 15–60 kg batteries [E]; abuse of modified "low-speed" scooters is a known enforcement issue.
- **3W/4W goods vehicles** (for truck batteries, truck tyres and barrels): transport registration (yellow plate), goods permit, commercial insurance, and a DL with the right class [E, standard MV Act practice]. Porter's 2W tier caps at 20 kg [P, Porter](https://porter.in/two-wheelers/jaipur).

### 2.6 DPDP Act 2023 and DPDP Rules 2025

- The Rules were notified 13–14 Nov 2025 and phase in over 18 months. Consent-manager rule from 13 Nov 2026; the main obligations (notice, security safeguards, breach reporting, retention, children) from **13 May 2027** [S, AZB](https://www.azbpartners.com/bank/update-indias-digital-personal-data-protection-framework-comes-into-effect/); [P, PIB](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/nov/doc20251117695301.pdf); [S, India Briefing](https://www.india-briefing.com/news/dpdp-rules-2025-india-data-protection-law-compliance-40769.html). Breach timing: "without delay" plus a detailed report; the "72 hours" figure is disputed [U].
- **Agent KYC data** (DL, RC, PAN, bank, selfie, location) is personal data processed for a "specified purpose". Give a clear notice and get consent. Retain only for the engagement plus the tax and audit period, then delete. Restrict staff access by role.
- **Customer data shared with agents** (name, phone, address, location) is processing by MechDost and should be scoped to the order and time-boxed. Agents are not MechDost's data processors in the usual sense, so the agent agreement must bar keeping, copying or using customer data. Mask the phone number and hide the address after delivery (3.1, 3.2).
- **Agent location tracking**: only while online or on a trip, with clear notice; no off-duty tracking [E]. This matches the DPDP purpose limitation and the project's consent rules (hard rule 16).

### 2.7 Aadhaar

- Private storage of Aadhaar numbers brings Aadhaar Data Vault expectations. UIDAI Circular 8 of 2025 (18 Jul 2025) limits ADV duties to Requesting Entities, but earlier clarifications reached employers storing masked numbers digitally [S, Khaitan](https://www.khaitanco.com/sites/default/files/2025-12/ERGO%20-%20Aadhaar%20Data%20Vault%20-%2010%20December%202025.pdf); [P, UIDAI ADV FAQ](https://www.uidai.gov.in/images/FAQs_Aadhaar_Data_Vault_03112025_v10.pdf).
- Misusing collected information carries up to 3 years' jail or a fine under s.40 [S, S.S. Rana](https://ssrana.in/articles/aadhaar-authentication-by-private-entities-from-data-privacy-perspective/).
- UIDAI announced (Dec 2025) that entities must register as Offline Verification Seeking Entities and use QR or app verification instead of photocopies. Gazette notification not seen [S, Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/india-news/uidai-to-mandate-registration-for-entities-using-aadhaar-verification-125120700522_1.html); [P, UIDAI OVSE](https://uidai.gov.in/en/ovse).
- Masked Aadhaar shows only the last 4 digits; the offline XML is signed and can be validated with UIDAI's public key [P, UIDAI](https://www.uidai.gov.in/283-faqs/aadhaar-online-services/e-aadhaar/1887-what-is-masked-aadhaar.html).
- **Recommendation:** do not collect Aadhaar at all. DL (government ID with photo and address) plus PAN covers identity and tax.

### 2.8 Hazardous goods: batteries and lubricants

- **CMVR Rule 129:** owners of goods carriages carrying dangerous goods must display class labels, carry safety equipment and, since 2022, fit an AIS-140 tracker [S/P, CMVR via cgtransport](https://cgtransport.gov.in/Notification/CMVR_1989.pdf); [P, MoRTH GSR 617(E)](<https://morth.gov.in/sites/default/files/notifications_document/6GSR%20617(E)%203rd%20august%202022%20VLT%20for%20dangerous%20hazardous%20goods%20vehicle.pdf>). Whether a few new automotive batteries on a 2W or 3W fall under it is **unverified** [U].
- **International reference:** ADR SP 598 takes new lead-acid batteries out of the dangerous-goods rules if they are secured against slipping, falling and damage, have carrying handles, and have no external acid [S, Danish authority](https://www.brs.dk/globalassets/brs---beredskabsstyrelsen/dokumenter/forebyggelse/2023/-sp598-2023-.pdf). This is European, not Indian law, but it is a sensible standard.
- **Courier practice abroad:** wet car batteries are banned by general couriers (UK, NZ, AU) [S, Evri/Post Office](https://www.postoffice.co.uk/mail/in-branch-evri-prohibited-items?campaignid=HNUNETWK); [S, NZ Couriers](https://help.nzcouriers.co.nz/dangerous-prohibited-goods/prohibited-items). Indian couriers' terms not checked [U].
- **Old batteries:** the Battery Waste Management Rules 2022 require waste batteries to go back through producer or EPR channels; consumers must dispose of them separately [S, Chambers](https://chambers.com/articles/new-rules-for-management-of-battery-waste-a-much-awaited-change); [P, PIB](https://pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1854433). Used batteries are hazardous waste [S, Nordic guidance]. **Don't let agents collect old batteries in Phase 1** [E].
- **Lubricants:** packaged engine oils are generally not dangerous goods for transport. Barrels (about 180–210 L, over 150 kg) are a manual-handling and vehicle-tier problem, not a hazmat one [E/U].
- **Safety SOP [E]:** batteries upright in the OEM box or a crate with straps; no transport of leaking or damaged units; gloves plus a baking-soda spill kit on 3W/4W; on a 2W, at most one battery per trip, only in a fixed carrier, and **nothing over 20 kg in total** (Porter's 2W cap). Heavier batteries, truck tyres and barrels go to a 3W or goods vehicle.

### 2.9 COD cash handling

- Common fraud: marking orders undelivered while keeping the cash; leakage found only at weekly reconciliation [V, Shipmozo](https://www.shipmozo.com/blog/top-cod-remittance-problems); [V, Shipsy](https://shipsy.ai/insights/quick-commerce-cod-management).
- Controls: a cash-in-hand cap of about 10–15 orders' worth, lower for new agents, daily reconciliation [V, EasySell](https://easysellapp.com/blogs/wiki/cod-courier-fraud-delivery-agent-theft); proof of remittance in-app, ban for skipping it [P, Borzo](https://borzodelivery.com/in/cod).
- **MechDost fit [E]:** the existing COD cap (₹5,000 default) and WhatsApp COD confirmation in `docs/decisions.md` still apply. Add: a UPI QR at the door (Razorpay QR or payment link), so cash is optional; a per-agent cash ledger; deposits by UPI to MechDost's account with a reference; a cap of about ₹10,000, or ₹5,000 for the first 2 weeks; and **no new COD assignment while over the cap or with an overdue deposit**. Whether the _shop_ or _MechDost_ receives the cash affects the existing "One shop money model" entry in `docs/decisions.md` (about line 603: the shop collects the customer total on COD), so it needs a new decisions entry. **Cap sizing:** the default per-order COD cap is ₹5,000 (`docs/decisions.md` ~line 65), and bulk-quote orders carry their own, possibly larger, COD ceiling (~line 602). An agent cap of ₹5,000 would therefore be blocked by one order. Set the agent cap at about 2–3× the per-order cap (₹10–15k), and send bulk-quote COD orders to `shop_self` or UPI-at-door only.

## 3. Customer privacy and safety

### 3.1 Number masking

- Documented harm: a Rapido rider kept calling a woman customer from several numbers after a ride (May 2026), and another messaged a customer on WhatsApp (Apr 2026). Masking existed, but the number still leaked or stayed reachable [S, OneIndia](https://www.oneindia.com/india/rapido-driver-allegedly-video-called-woman-from-bathroom-after-ride-passenger-claims-days-of-harass-8082249.html); [S, The Logical Indian](https://thelogicalindian.com/ghaziabad-woman-shares-rapido-riders-inappropriate-whatsapp-messages-sparking-debate-on-passenger-safety/). A Zepto delivery partner was arrested for molesting a woman customer [S, Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/zepto-delivery-partner-molests-woman-in-mumbai-startup-promises-stringent-action).
- **Providers and cost:**
  - Exotel prices by quote: per pulse and per leg (a bridged call has 2 legs), plus monthly rental per number, with volume tiers [P, Exotel blog](https://exotel.com/blog/voice-call-api-in-india/); [P, Exotel billing](https://developer.exotel.com/docs/billing/enterprise-billing).
  - Competitor claims: Exotel about ₹0.50–1.50 a minute; Edesy ₹1.50 a minute all-in; Exotel accounts carry ₹5–15k a month in platform and rental fees [V, Edesy](https://edesy.in/number-masking/compare/top-5-number-masking-providers).
  - Knowlarity, Plivo and Ozonetel: no prices found [U].
  - **Estimate [E]:** about 3 masked minutes per order is ₹2–5 per order, plus a ₹2–15k monthly floor. At 20–50 orders a day, masking costs roughly ₹4–15k a month.
- **Cheaper Phase-1 alternative [E]:** in-app "call customer" through a bridged call from a small pool of virtual numbers, tied to the order and shut off after it is delivered or cancelled plus a grace period. Or route calls through the MechDost support line (7978776551) with a click-to-call bridge.

### 3.2 Address and contact exposure rules (recommended [E])

| Stage                                       | Agent sees                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Offer (before accept)                       | Shop area, drop **locality/PIN** and distance, weight and vehicle tier, COD amount (yes/no) |
| Accepted, going to shop                     | Shop name, address, masked call to shop                                                     |
| **After pickup is confirmed**               | Customer first name, exact address, landmark, map pin, masked call button                   |
| After delivered/cancelled plus about 30 min | Everything customer-related hidden; history shows the order code and locality only          |

Never show the customer's raw phone number. Never let the customer see the agent's raw number either (both sides masked). Show the customer the agent's first name, photo, vehicle number and live location only between pickup and delivery.

### 3.3 Fraud and safety threats

| Threat                                                                         | Mitigation                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Agent impersonation** (someone else rides on a verified ID)                  | Selfie with liveness at each go-online, or randomly; photo shown to the customer; the shop checks the face at pickup                                                                                                                         |
| **Fake delivery** (marked delivered, never handed over)                        | Delivery only with the customer's 4-digit code (already MechDost's rule); geofence check at drop; proof photo; customer "not received" flow inside 24 h                                                                                      |
| **OTP/code fraud** (agent phishes the code by phone; customer shares it early) | The code shows only in the customer's app and WhatsApp, with "share only when the parcel is in your hands"; rate-limit code attempts; flag deliveries far from the drop pin                                                                  |
| **COD theft or absconding**                                                    | 2.9 controls; cash cap; daily deposit; a deposit or bond for new agents (Borzo-style "parcel security fee", [P]) is legal but deters joining, so a lower cap is the better alternative                                                       |
| **Parcel swap or tampering** (fake battery swapped for a real one)             | Shop seals the parcel with a MechDost tamper tape or sticker with the order code; **pickup photo** of the sealed parcel plus battery serial/barcode scan; customer checks seal and serial on delivery; warranty card serial matched to order |
| **Collusion** (shop plus agent, or agent plus customer, faking "not received") | Audit trail; patterns by agent and shop; random customer calls                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Fake GPS**                                                                   | Mock-location detection on Android; speed and teleport checks                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Account sharing or selling**                                                 | Device binding plus periodic selfie                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Women customers' safety**                                                    | Masking both ways; contact cut after delivery; one-tap "report agent" with an immediate suspension path (Telangana allows no notice for "imminent physical danger"); late-evening delivery window configurable by admin                      |
| **Agent safety**                                                               | SOS button that shares location with MechDost ops and an emergency contact; no forced deliveries after a configurable hour; refuse unsafe loads                                                                                              |

### 3.4 Location tracking consent

Track agents only while online or on a delivery, with an in-app notice and explicit consent, and keep fine-grained location for a short time (for example 30–90 days, then aggregate) [E, aligned with DPDP purpose limitation]. Customers see the agent only during the active delivery. Bengaluru police want GPS delivery logs from q-commerce firms [S, Deccan Herald], so keep trip logs long enough for police requests (a set retention, documented).

| Group personal accident (₹5–10 lakh)                             | ₹450–1,800 per person per year [V]                                         | ₹15–55k/year for 30 agents                                                                           |
| Welfare/social-security contingency                              | 1–2% of turnover, capped at 5% of payouts [P]                              | Budget **up to 5% of agent payouts** (the cap binds for a goods marketplace) until a lawyer confirms |
| Uniform/bag/T-shirt                                              | Swiggy-style ₹600–1,500 deposit [V]                                        | Give a MechDost T-shirt and ID card free; it builds trust at the door                                |

**Comparison point:** outsourcing a ≤20 kg, ~5 km drop to Porter or Shiprocket Quick in a similar city probably costs the merchant about ₹50–90 (Porter ₹30 base for 1 km plus per-km; Shiprocket "from ₹10/km") [P/V, E]. Own agents only beat that once each one does about 8+ drops a day.

## 5. Risks and recommended phasing

### 5.1 Risk register

| #   | Risk                                                                                                                     | Type                | Likelihood / impact | Mitigation                                                                                                                                                          |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------- | ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| R1  | Agent injured carrying a heavy battery or in a road accident; no cover; claim refused (private policy used commercially) | Legal / human       | Med / High          | GPA cover for every agent from day 1; require a declared or commercial vehicle policy; vehicle-tier rules; weight caps per tier; handling SOP                       |
| R2  | Agents held to be employees (EPF/ESI, wages, PoSH)                                                                       | Legal               | Low–Med / High      | Contractor-friendly design (2.3); right to refuse; no fixed shifts; partner agreement reviewed by a lawyer; consider a 3PL for heavy freight                        |
| R3  | Aggregator contribution and eShram non-compliance                                                                        | Legal               | Med / Med           | Register on eShram; upload agents; budget up to 5% of agent payouts; follow notifications                                                                           |

### 5.2 Recommended phased approach

**Phase 1: 10–30 agents in Raipur (build first)**

1. **Application form** (public, with Turnstile): name, phone, city, vehicle tier (2W / 3W / goods), DL number + DOB, RC number, PAN, bank; selfie; consents (DPDP notice, location while online). **No Aadhaar.**
2. **Admin review queue**: API checks (DL, RC, PAN, bank penny drop); police character certificate upload (CG Police portal); approve or reject with a reason code. **Invite** by email plus WhatsApp: a single-use link that leads to phone-OTP sign-in, then the role `delivery_agent`.
3. **Agent web app/PWA** (mobile first, Hindi plus English): online/offline toggle; assigned-trip list; trip screen; earnings; documents; SOS; help.
4. **Dispatch = admin-assigned**, with suggestions filtered by vehicle tier ≥ order's heaviest item, online status and distance. One active assignment per order (partial unique index, as the hard rules require). Agent accepts or declines with a short timer; on timeout or decline the order goes back to admin.
5. **Trip per sub-order:** orders split into one sub-order per shop (`docs/decisions.md` ~lines 235, 583), so the trip and the "one active assignment" rule attach to the **sub-order**, not the parent order. **Trip state machine:** `assigned → accepted → at_shop → picked_up (shop confirms + sealed-parcel photo + battery serial) → at_drop → delivered (customer code) | failed (reason code) → returned_to_shop`. Each step is a conditional UPDATE with version, events and audit.
6. **Privacy rules from 3.2**; masked calling through one provider (pilot), or a bridged call through the support line.
7. **Delivery mode per order: `mechdost_agent` (default) | `shop_self` (shop opted in, admin-approved) | `third_party` (Porter etc. for heavy items).** All three end with the same delivery-code rule.
8. **COD:** UPI QR at the door preferred; cash ledger, cap, daily deposit, block when over the cap.
9. **Payouts:** admin-configured rate cards per city × vehicle tier (base, per km beyond X, heavy-item handling, waiting, incentive); weekly payout statement in integer paise; TDS at 1% once thresholds are crossed (20% if no PAN); export for manual bank transfer (RazorpayX payouts later).
10. **Ratings and deactivation:** the customer rates the delivery; the agent can rate the shop's handover. Suspension is immediate for safety or fraud; otherwise 7-day notice plus appeal. Rules published on an agent-facing "How dispatch and pay work" page (Telangana s.14 style).
11. **Compliance basics:** partner agreement (lawyer review), GPA insurance policy, eShram aggregator registration, a written handling SOP for batteries and tyres, a PoSH-style complaints route.

**Phase 2 (30–100 agents, or a second city)**

- Auto-offer to the nearest eligible agent with an accept timer (~30–60 s), then the next one; fallback to admin.
- Batching small parts from one shop; multi-drop.
- Native app (Android first) for background location, mock-GPS detection and better camera and barcode capture.
- Instant or daily payouts through RazorpayX; incentive campaigns; minimum-earnings guarantees by zone and time.
- Number-masking at scale through a negotiated contract.
- Per-state compliance settings (welfare fee %, notice period) driven by city config.
- Old-battery take-back pickups, only through an authorised recycler or brand SOP.

**Phase 3**

- Fleet partners (3PL/Porter API) as a fallback lane in the dispatch engine; ETA prediction; fraud scoring; agent training modules and certification for heavy goods.

## 6. Open questions to queue for the user (suggested defaults)

1. **Who holds COD cash: shop, or MechDost through the agent?** Default: the agent collects and deposits to MechDost; the shop's payout is unchanged. Needs a decisions entry because it changes the "One shop money model" entry (`docs/decisions.md` ~line 603).
2. **Heavy items (more than 20 kg, or truck sizes):** own 3W agents, or Porter from day 1? Default: Porter or shop self-delivery for heavy items in Phase 1; own agents for ≤20 kg.
3. **GST model for the delivery fee (s.9(5) ECO vs own supply):** default: ask the CA before charging a separate delivery fee from agents' work.
4. **Insurance:** buy GPA (₹5–10 lakh) for all active agents? Default: yes, before the first live delivery.
5. **Police verification required for every agent?** Default: yes (reputational safety, MVAG benchmark).
6. **Rate card numbers for Raipur:** default from section 4, to be confirmed after talking to 5–10 local riders.
7. **Lawyer review** of the partner agreement, eShram registration and the white-plate bike question with the CG transport department.

# Ustaad Network: security, safety and compliance review (design stage)

Date: 2026-11-26 · Scope: the Ustaad Network as designed in `product-study.md`, `research-legal-safety.md`, `research-new-features.md`, `ustaad-workflow.html`, `ustaad-admin.html` and the prototype `src/*.js`. It is checked against the repo's hard rules (`CLAUDE.md` 1–16), `docs/specs/13-rbac-security-abuse.md` and `docs/research/security-vulnerabilities.md`. No Ustaad code exists yet, so this is a design review. Nothing was built or run.

**How this was produced (honest method note)**

- `security-review` skill: invoked. It reviews a git diff, and `main` is clean with no diff, so it had nothing to review and produced no code findings. Its categories (injection, authZ, crypto, data exposure) are applied below to the design.
- `claude-security:attack-surface-analysis`: invoked. Its pipeline needs a pinned checkout, stage agents and no network access, so it does not fit a design with no code, and its no-network rule conflicts with this brief's research request. Its three lenses were applied by hand instead: default attack surface (what ships on by default but is not needed), early reachability (what is reachable before verification or authentication) and configuration interactions (admin settings that together switch off a protection). See §1.4.
- `anthropic-skills:deep-research`: invoked. Two researchers verified the legal points. Their notes are in `research_notes/Ustaad India compliance gaps/` (`it_rules_pocso.md`, `consumer_labour_ip.md`). The separate report-writer step was skipped so that this file is the only deliverable. **No Gazette, IndiaCode or PIB page could be read in full in the time box.** Legal claims are therefore marked **[S]** (secondary: law firm, legal press) unless stated otherwise. **[P]** is used only where a primary document URL is the cited source. That is still a search-level reading, so everything legal is "confirm with counsel".
- OWASP, NIST and Unicode TR39 references are cited from standing knowledge of those pages. They were not fetched in this session. Location-privacy and NCRB/NCMEC sources come from web searches run in this session.
- Prototype facts come from `src/app-admin.js` (the in-app defaults) and from `ustaad-admin.html` (the admin page). **The two disagree** in places, and those disagreements are listed in §2 (D19).
- `mechdost-advisor` was **not** run in this sub-task (hard rule 11). The product study's advisor brief (§0) was reused. The caller should run the advisor before building.

## 1. Threat model

### 1.1 System sketch (components referred to below)

- **C1 Auth**: Firebase phone OTP, then password, custom claims, staff 2FA.
- **C2 Verification**: garage-owner attestation, Ustaad vouch, staff final approval.
- **C3 API**: Hono routes, Zod, actor-scoped repositories, RLS mirror.
- **C4 Media**: signed upload, quarantine bucket, scan/transcode workers, then the public-to-members bucket.
- **C5 Link preview worker**.
- **C6 Feed/ranker, Discover, hashtags**.
- **C7 Location**: garage pin, zone counts, distance.
- **C8 Social graph and DMs**.
- **C9 Q&A, Izzat, levels, Coins**.
- **C10 Jobs and mentorship**.
- **C11 Smart correction and speech-to-text**.
- **C12 Moderation console** (`community_manager`), takedown service and grievance desk.
- **C13 Notifications**: outbox, WhatsApp share.
- **C14 Promotions** ("Sponsored").
- **C15 Admin settings**.

### 1.3 Location privacy design (T7, in detail)

Prototype facts: `PEOPLE.*.km` is shown with one decimal ("3.6 km away"). The Discover "near" list is **sorted by km**. Zones show the exact number from 10 (`exactFrom: 10`), and round to 5 only above 20. A per-speciality breakdown is shown for zones of 10 or more, including cells of **1** (e.g. `ev: 1` in zone a5, `cv: 1` in a1). `ZONE_MIN = 5` is hard-coded beside the admin `band`. Trilateration from displayed or filterable distance has repeatedly located dating-app users to within 2–111 m, even with rounding ([Check Point Research 2024](https://research.checkpoint.com/2024/the-illusion-of-privacy-geolocation-risks-in-modern-dating-apps/); [KU Leuven, reported by TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/07/31/bumble-and-hinge-allowed-stalkers-to-pinpoint-users-locations-down-to-2-meters-researchers-say); [arXiv 1605.06533](https://ar5iv.arxiv.org/html/1605.06533)).

Requirements:

1. **Distance is computed server-side from the viewer's stored garage pin**, never from a client-supplied point. The viewer pin can change at most 3 times a day (moving your own pin is the multilateration tool).
2. Show only **bands**: "< 2 km", "2–5 km", "5–10 km", "10+ km". Add **stable per-pair noise** before banding (HMAC(pair, secret) jitter of ±500 m), so repeated queries do not average it out.
3. **No sorting within a band**. Shuffle within a band per day. The "near" list orders by band, then by relevance.
4. **No distance filter smaller than 2 km** (oracle trilateration).
5. Zone counts: suppress below k = 5 ("a few"). Band 5–9. Round to the nearest 5 at every count of 10 or more (not only above 20). **Suppress speciality cells below k = 5** and complementary cells (so a total minus visible cells does not reveal a hidden one).
6. Counts are recomputed **nightly, not live** (joining and leaving cannot be watched as differences). Zones are admin-defined polygons of at least about 2 km².
7. Exact pin: opt-in. Visible only to **accepted connections**, not to followers. Shown on the map with a 50 m display fuzz. **Never on stories or posts** (posts carry the area only). Women and anyone else can hide their location entirely with one switch, and hidden users are excluded from zone counts (exclusion must not be inferable at the band level).
8. Location history is not kept. Only the current pin, with `updated_at`.
9. Tests: the trilateration acceptance test (AT-LOC-1) in §4.

### 1.4 The three attack-surface lenses applied to the design

- **Default surface (what is on but not needed)**: all 10 post types, stories, DMs, jobs, links and documents ship on by default (`posts.types.*: true`, `jobs.on: true`, `promo.on: true`). Recommendation: keep the founder's "everything", but per city cohort make **documents, links and DMs** wait for their hardening controls (SR-20 to SR-34) before switching on. Stories have no "seen by" list by default.
- **Early reachability (before verification or authentication)**: the prototype gates the zone map behind `canZone()` (verified or Ustaad). The same gate must apply server-side to Discover, profiles, media URLs, link-preview fetches and **share teasers** (T32). The WhatsApp share page is the only unauthenticated surface, so it needs Turnstile-free rate limits and no member data.
- **Configuration interactions (admin settings that together defeat a protection)**:
  - `ver.staff: false` together with followers-based level rules gives fully automatic Bada/Master levels, so farming pays out.
  - `loc.band` set lower than `ZONE_MIN`, or `exactFrom` ≤ `band`, removes k-anonymity.
  - An editable `promo.label` plus `promo.every: 1` produces an undisclosed-advertisement dark pattern.
  - `sc.mode: 'auto'` with an empty protect list lets meaning change silently.
  - `join: 'all'` with `ver.owner: false`, `ver.vouch: false` and staff approval off means anyone can become an Ustaad.
  - `posts.vidMax` with no hard ceiling means unbounded cost.

  Each needs a **server-enforced invariant** (SR-40).

## 4. Security requirements (testable) and acceptance tests

### 4.1 Requirements

**Identity and accounts**

- **SR-1** Ustaad routes require an authenticated, phone-verified user with `verifyIdToken(checkRevoked)`. Unverified members can read only their own profile and the verification flow (403 `USTAAD_UNVERIFIED` elsewhere).
- **SR-2** Phone change, password reset or a new-device sign-in for a Pakka+ account triggers re-OTP, notifies the old channels, and freezes perks and job posting for 72 h.
- **SR-3** Turnstile is verified server-side at Ustaad sign-up and on the first post, first DM and first job.
- **SR-4** Reserved names: display names and handles are NFKC-folded and confusable-skeleton matched (TR39) against a reserved list (MechDost, staff, brands). A match gets 422 `NAME_RESERVED`.
- **SR-5** The official/staff badge comes only from a server-side flag. No client field can set it (strict Zod rejects unknown fields).

**Verification and levels**

- **SR-6** An owner attestation is valid only if the owner is a verified workshop partner, or has GSTIN/Udyam plus a geotagged shop photo within 200 m of the pin. At most 10 attestations per owner per quarter.
- **SR-7** A vouch needs a voucher who is Pakka+, at least 60 days old, not reciprocal, with no more than 3 vouches a month. A verification needs vouchers from ≥ 2 distinct workplaces.
- **SR-8** The final step of verification, and every promotion to Bada or Master, requires a staff approval row written by the API (a state-machine transition with `version`). No admin setting can bypass it.
- **SR-9** Qualified followers (verified, ≥ 30 days, distinct workplace, not in a flagged ring) are the only followers counted for levels. The calculation is in a pure, unit-tested function. This is a **proposal** for the founder to confirm: it keeps followers in the rules, per the ruling, but defines which followers count.
- **SR-10** The nightly ring job flags pairs with more than 30% reciprocal likes, votes or follows, and dense subgraphs. Flagged accounts' votes weigh 0 until reviewed.
- **SR-11** Perk and Coin multipliers are evaluated at use time against the current level and the absence of active strikes.

**Content, uploads and media**

- **SR-12** All UGC media is uploaded through a signed URL bound to the user, the declared kind, a maximum size and a 10-minute expiry, into a **quarantine bucket**. Nothing in quarantine is readable by members.
- **SR-13** The media worker checks the type by magic bytes. Images are re-encoded to WebP with all metadata gone. Audio is transcoded to Opus. Video is transcoded to H.264 MP4 (480p/720p) with container metadata stripped. Originals are deleted.
- **SR-14** ffmpeg runs in a no-network container with `-protocol_whitelist file,pipe`, a 120 s timeout, a memory cap and an output-size cap. Playlist, concat and HLS inputs are refused.
- **SR-15** Video longer than `min(admin vidMax, 180 s)` is refused before transcoding (probe first). Size: a 180 s phone clip at 720p–1080p is typically 60–250 MB. So the client compresses to about 720p / 2.5 Mbps before upload (about 60 MB for 180 s), using a **resumable** (tus/multipart) upload with a hard server cap of 150 MB. Anything over the cap is refused with "compress and retry" in en + hi.
- **SR-16** Documents: PDF only. Scanned for malware. Sanitised (JavaScript, embedded files, actions and forms removed) or rasterised. Served from a separate cookieless origin with `Content-Disposition: attachment`, `nosniff` and `CSP: sandbox`. Never rendered inline on the main origin.
- **SR-17** SVG, HTML, archives and executables are refused for every kind (422).
- **SR-18** Every image and video keyframe is hash-matched against the CSAM list and NSFW-scored before publishing. On a match, publishing is blocked, the file goes to the evidence vault, a POCSO report task is created, and the account is frozen.
- **SR-19** Faces and number plates are auto-blurred, and phone numbers detected by OCR are blocked, before publishing. The author can undo a blur box only for their own face.
- **SR-20** Per-user daily upload quota and a site-wide transcode budget, both admin-set within hard ceilings. When the budget is exhausted, uploads fail closed with a friendly message.
- **SR-21** Media is read through signed URLs valid ≤ 10 min, issued after an authorisation check (member, not blocked, content not removed, story not expired).

**Links and text**

- **SR-22** Link previews run in an isolated worker. http/https only, ports 80/443. The name is resolved, the address checked against a private/loopback/link-local/CGNAT/ULA/metadata deny list, and the connection made to the pinned IP. At most 3 redirects, each re-checked. 3 s total timeout. 512 KB body. `text/html` only. No cookies.
- **SR-23** The link host is shown as the registrable domain parsed with WHATWG URL. IDN shown in punycode. Links outside the allow-list go through a warning page with an HMAC-signed target (no open redirect).
- **SR-24** Links are checked against Web Risk/Safe Browsing at post time and rechecked daily. A malicious result hides the post.
- **SR-25** Hashtags match `^[\p{L}\p{N}_]{1,40}$` after NFC. Blocked-tag list (illegal modification, abuse). At most 10 per post.
- **SR-26** No UGC is rendered with `dangerouslySetInnerHTML`. The CSP has nonces and no `unsafe-inline`.
- **SR-27** Smart correction never auto-applies a change that alters a digit, unit, part code or protected term. The original is stored and shown one tap away. Auto-fixed text carries an "edited" marker.
- **SR-28** Any text sent to a third-party processor (STT, LLM) has phone numbers, plates and names redacted, goes only to a contracted processor in an India region (or on-device), and is logged without the content.

**Location**

- **SR-29** Distance is computed server-side from stored pins only, banded (< 2, 2–5, 5–10, 10+ km) after stable per-pair noise, and never returned as a number.
- **SR-30** A viewer's own pin can change at most 3 times in 24 h and 6 times in 30 days, and each move over 1 km needs a GPS fix with an accuracy of 50 m or better. No distance filter below 2 km. No ordering within a band.
- **SR-31** Zone and speciality counts suppress cells below k = 5 (and complementary cells), round counts of 10 or more to 5, and refresh nightly. `loc.k` is server-clamped to ≥ 5.
- **SR-32** The exact pin is returned only to accepted connections of a user who opted in, with 50 m display fuzz. Users with location hidden return no area, no distance and no zone membership.

**Social graph, DMs, jobs**

- **SR-33** DMs are request-only for everyone by default. No links or media before acceptance. Accounts under 7 days cannot start a DM. At most 20 new threads a day.
- **SR-34** The phone number never appears in any Ustaad API response except a value the owner typed into a DM.
- **SR-35** Blocking returns 404 for the blocked user on profile, posts, media and DMs.
- **SR-36** Job posts need a verified employer, the no-minors attestation and structured fields. Phone, UPI or a fee phrase in the body holds the post for review. Candidates can never be charged.
- **SR-37** Discover and search: 20 per page, cursor only, no total count, 200 profiles per day per user. No bulk export for any role.

**Moderation, staff and admin**

- **SR-38** `community_manager` holds `community:moderate` and `community:verify`, city scoped, staff 2FA. It has no `customers:read_pii` and no `exports:run`. Reading DM content requires a report id plus a reason and writes an audit event.
- **SR-39** Takedown service: one action disables access across the feed, profile, search, share teasers and signed URLs within 60 s. The content and records are kept for 180 days. Each queue item carries an SLA timer by reason code (2 h, 3 h, 36 h, 7 days).
- **SR-40** Admin settings validate invariants server-side:
  - staff approval cannot be turned off;
  - `loc.k` ≥ 5;
  - `exactFrom` > `band`;
  - the promotion label comes from an ASCI-permitted list;
  - `promo.every` ≥ 8;
  - `vidMax` ≤ 180;
  - `sc.mode: 'auto'` requires a non-empty protect list.

  A failing setting gets 422 `SETTING_INVARIANT`. Every change is audited. The first two (staff approval, label) are locked because rulings require them. The others are bounds proposed for the founder to confirm: a super_admin can move them with a reason code and an audit entry (§0).

- **SR-41** Promotions come only from the promotions service with `content_type = sponsored | mechdost_authored`, are never treated as UGC, and carry the label in the first line.
- **SR-42** Every Ustaad action emits a typed analytics event (hard rule 9), pseudonymous. When a minor is reported, analytics and recommendations for that account switch off.
- **SR-43** Rate limits (Redis) per user and IP:
  - posts 3 a day per type (admin `perDay`);
  - comments 30 an hour;
  - likes 100 a day;
  - follows 20 a day;
  - reports 20 a day.

  Over-limit responses are 429 with headers.

- **SR-44** WhatsApp share pages show a generic teaser only (no post text, media or names), are rate limited per IP, and carry `noindex`.
- **SR-46** Chhattisgarh only: Ustaad sign-up and verification require a garage pin inside the admin-enabled Chhattisgarh cities (PostGIS `ST_Within` on city polygons). Every Ustaad row carries `city_id` (hard rule 7). Feed, Discover and zone queries filter by the enabled cities. A pin outside them gets 422 `USTAAD_REGION`.
- **SR-45** ICT logs are kept 180 days in India. Incident runbooks for 6 h (CERT-In) and 72 h (DPDP) are tested by a tabletop drill before launch.

**Abuse and logic tests**

## 5. Top 15 must-fix before launch (ranked)

1. **CSAM and child-safety pipeline**: hash matching before publishing, an evidence vault, POCSO s.19/20 + Rule 11 reporting to cybercrime.gov.in, moderator protection. This is the most severe consequence with no age check (T14, L15, SR-18).
2. **Takedown and grievance operations at the 2026 clocks**: 3 h for orders, 2 h for intimate imagery and impersonation, 36 h and 7 days; a named Grievance Officer; a 24×7 rota; a one-click disable that keeps evidence. Otherwise safe harbour is lost (T33, L2–L4, SR-39).
3. **Location privacy**: banded, noisy, server-computed distance; no sort within bands; limited pin changes; k ≥ 5 with speciality-cell suppression; nightly counts. The prototype's "3.6 km" and the km sort allow trilateration (T7, D5, SR-29–32).
4. **Upload pipeline for audio, video and PDF**: quarantine, a sandboxed ffmpeg, PDF sanitising on a separate origin, quotas and transcode budget. Spec 13 covers only JPEG/PNG/WebP today (T16–T19, SR-12–17, SR-20).
5. **SSRF-safe link-preview worker** with an IP deny list and pinned connection (IMDS on the AWS host is the prize), plus correct host display (T21–T22, SR-22–23).
6. **Staff final approval as a non-disableable invariant**, plus qualified-follower counting and ring detection. The admin page's `staffFinal` switch ("Without it, proofs approve automatically") and the follower thresholds together pay out farming (D1–D3, SR-8–10, SR-40).
7. **Request-only DMs and hidden phone for everyone**, with no gender field and new-account DM limits (T8, D8, SR-33–34).
8. **`community_manager` role**: city-scoped, 2FA, no PII and no exports, audited DM access, plus its role-leak matrix (T25, SR-38).
9. **Scam controls on jobs, DMs and comments**: no-fee rule, verified employers, phrase and UPI/OTP filters, Web Risk link checks, no links for new accounts (T4, D11, SR-24, SR-36).
10. **Minor-reported restricted mode and no-minors job attestation**, as the compensating control for the no-age-check ruling, plus counsel on DPDP s.9 (§0, T15, L11, L19).
11. **Locked "Sponsored/प्रायोजित" label and promotion floor**, enforced server-side, replacing the editable "MechDost offer" (T31, D4, L17–18, SR-41).
12. **Media authorisation**: signed ≤ 10 min URLs after auth, random keys, story expiry enforced at signing, blocked users get 404, RLS mirror on all Ustaad tables (T23, SR-21, SR-35).

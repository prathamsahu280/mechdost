# Boundary geometry available for free (Raipur) - research 2026-11-28

Summary: **one usable PIN polygon source exists and works today** (BharatViz copy of the Department of Posts 2025 pincode boundary GeoJSON). Ward polygons and Nava Raipur sector polygons do not exist in any free source found.

## 1. PIN-code polygons

### 1.1 BEST: Department of Posts "All India Pincode Boundary Geo JSON" via a BharatViz mirror

- Original: data.gov.in https://www.data.gov.in/catalog/all-india-pincode-boundary-geo-json and AIKosh https://aikosh.indiaai.gov.in/home/datasets/details/all_india_pincode_boundary_geo_json.html. Publisher: Ministry of Communications, Department of Posts. Licence on the AIKosh page: **Open Government License - India (GODL-India)**, which allows commercial use with attribution. The original download has been **404 since ~May 2026** (datameet thread https://groups.google.com/g/datameet/c/8iWJIsK-Y0w, 12 May 2026). The AIKosh page metadata still shows the dataset (dated 30/06/25, last updated 26 Nov 2026) with file size 0.
- Mirror that works: **https://geo.bharatviz.org/geojsons/pincodes/chhattisgarh.geojson** (15.9 MB, `Last-Modified` 15 Aug 2026, CORS `*`). Per-state files exist for every state; whole India: `https://geo.bharatviz.org/geojsons/pincodes/India_pincodes_simplified.geojson` (not downloaded; the older `bharatviz.org/India_pincodes_simplified.geojson` link in the datameet thread returns the HTML app, not data). The URLs were read from the BharatViz web bundle (`https://bharatviz.org/assets/index-*.js`). Download: `curl -A "mechdost-research/1.0" -O https://geo.bharatviz.org/geojsons/pincodes/chhattisgarh.geojson`.
- Format: FeatureCollection, MultiPolygon, WGS84. Properties: `pincode, office_name, state_name, district_name`. 1,280 features for Chhattisgarh; one feature **per delivery post office/district combination**, so a PIN shared by several districts repeats (geometry identical per PIN in the cases checked).
- Licence of the mirror: the BharatViz code repo https://github.com/saketlab/bharatviz is MIT; **no explicit data licence is stated for the GeoJSON files**. The underlying data is GODL-India (commercial use allowed with attribution "Department of Posts, Government of India / data.gov.in"). Action: attribute DoP, note the BharatViz mirror, and ask the maintainer (Saket Choudhary, saketlab) to confirm before shipping the polygons in the product. It is a simplified copy ("original was over 300 MB" per the datameet thread); the full-resolution original is not currently available.
- **Raipur coverage verified.** Polygons present for 492001, 492002, 492003 (Urla), 492004, 492005, 492008, 492009, 492010, 492012, 492013, 492014, 492015, 492016, 492018, 492099, 492101, 493111, 493221. **Missing: 492006, 492007, 492011, 492017** (no India Post office in the 2025 directory/polygons carries these PINs; older sites list some of them - see README conflicts).
- Quality: **simplified** (e.g. 492001 has only 62 vertices, 492099 has 153). Edges are approximate (up to hundreds of metres); 492002 and 492005 are tiny rectangles (point-like, around the Mantralaya and Vidhan Sabha offices), not areas.
- **Ground-truth test passed:** the GeoNames point for Sarona (21.24481 N, 81.57505 E) falls inside the 492099 polygon (founder: Sarona = 492099; OSM Nominatim says 493332). AIIMS Raipur (Tatibandh) also falls in 492099.
- A trimmed copy with only the 36 Raipur-district features for the live PINs is in `raipur-pin-polygons-dop2025-simplified.geojson` (42 KB).

## 2. Ward polygons (Raipur Municipal Corporation, 70 wards, 10 zones in the Wikipedia table)

- **OSM:** an Overpass query for `boundary=administrative` relations with `admin_level` 9, 10 or 11 inside 21.10-21.35 N, 81.55-81.80 E (2026-11-28) returned **zero** relations. OSM has no Raipur ward polygons; it only has state (4), district (5) and tahsil (6) relations.
- **DataMeet Municipal Spatial Data** https://projects.datameet.org/Municipal_Spatial_Data/ (CC BY 4.0, GeoJSON): only Bangalore, Ahmedabad, Bhopal, Bhubaneswar, Pune. **No Raipur.**
- Not verified to hold Raipur wards (metadata only): DataMeet thread https://groups.google.com/g/datameet/c/k7Hp9jEDRTk and NATMO https://geoportal.natmo.gov.in/dataset/raipur. RMC portal https://mcraipur.in/ was not checked for GIS; request the ward-boundary shapefile (2019/2025 delimitation) from RMC under RTI.
- Wikipedia ward names are person names (e.g. "Veer Savarkar Nagar Ward"), not localities, and the table lists only 57 of 70 wards: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raipur_Municipal_Corporation. Census 2011 ward pages (72 wards) are attribute-only: https://www.censusindia2011.com/chhattisgarh/raipur/raipur/raipur-m-corp-og-population.html

## 3. District / tehsil boundaries

- OSM relations (ODbL), found by Overpass 2026-11-28: Raipur district r1972682, Raipur Tahsil r9903142, Abhanpur Tahsil r9903144, Arang Tahsil r9903140, Tilda Tahsil r9903137, Patan Tahsil r9906889, Durg r1972688, Bemetara r9524220. Download: https://www.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/relation/9903142/full or Overpass `rel(9903142);out geom;`. ODbL allows commercial use with attribution and share-alike on the derived database.
- Not fetched: Survey of India / Bharat Maps https://bharatmaps.gov.in (terms restrict redistribution: treat as not usable); LGD https://lgdirectory.gov.in (codes, not geometry); BharatViz districts `https://geo.bharatviz.org/geojsons/districts/India_LGD_districts.geojson` and village layers `https://geo.bharatviz.org/geojsons/admin/villages-nwdp/chhattisgarh.geojson` (path pattern read from the bundle; licence unstated; worth a look for village polygons).

## 4. Nava Raipur (Atal Nagar) sectors

- OSM has only **points**: place=neighbourhood Sector 7, 19-22, 25-30, place=suburb Sector 2 and 3, "Capital Complex", and the city way w1104666684 (Atal Nagar-Nava Raipur). No sector polygons.
- NRDA / ANVP master plan 2031 (237.42 sq km, 41 villages): site navaraipuratalnagar.com; no GIS download found. 1acre.in has a third-party layer https://1acre.in/map-layers/chhattisgarh/new-raipur-masterplan (boundary only, licence unknown, not usable without permission).
- Polygon facts: **492018 "Sector 27 Naya Raipur SO"** (bbox 81.73-81.84 E, 21.129-21.188 N) covers most of the Nava Raipur sectors and the Capital Complex; **492002** is a tiny rectangle at (21.16 N, 81.797 E) next to it; sectors 2, 3 and 7 fall in 492101 (Mandir Hasaud).

## 5. Recommendation

Use the DoP 2025 polygons as the base PIN layer (best available, official origin, GODL-India). Ship the simplified copy only after confirming the mirror's terms, and refine edges with verified locality points (README method). Ask India Post / data.gov.in for the full-resolution file if it reappears.

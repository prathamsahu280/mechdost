What changed (look only)

  • Hero: white canvas on the left with a single strong headline; the second line is muted instead of orange, so the orange is saved for the one button that matters.
  • Emoji replaced by consistent line icons (categories, trust points, announcement).
  • Product cards: photo on a fine engineering grid with the part reference in the corner (the live cards already carry a part reference); price in large tabular figures over a dashed rule.
  • Sections sit on a hairline rule with a sentence-case label, instead of tracked ALL-CAPS eyebrows.
  • The two step lists ("How your order reaches you", "Know the work") are numbered because they are real sequences.
  • Header and dock: same items as the approved v4; the active tab is marked with an orange underline.

What changed and why

  • Map + "where is the vehicle?" first, with one-tap "use my location" — the service flow already needs the location before anything else.
  • "What does your Activa need?" as 6 tiles with a starting price, including "Not sure — we'll check" for people who can't name the problem.
  • Workshop cards show what decides the choice: rating, distance, open now, verified, pickup, and a price range.
  • The 3-step "how it works" (approve the estimate, pay after) is the trust promise; kept short.
  • Booking on one screen: saved vehicle, day chips, time slots (taken ones crossed out), pickup switch, and an expected-cost table. One button: "Request booking".

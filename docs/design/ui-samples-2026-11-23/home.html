What changed and why

  • Location line first (like the service apps people already use): "Raipur 492001 · Delivery across Chhattisgarh · Cash on delivery" answers the three trust questions before anything else.
  • Search with voice at the top; Hindi voice search helps mechanics and older customers who don't like typing.
  • "Shopping for" vehicle card with a number plate (CG 04 …): one tap shows parts that fit. Ties into "My garage".
  • 8 big category tiles instead of long text sections; easy to tap, readable in Hindi.
  • Products on the first screen, 2 per row, with real photos, rating, MRP vs price and a clear button.
  • Today's 12-screen home is cut to ~4 screens: the long explainers become one service banner and three trust rows.
  • 5-tab dock: Home, Shop, Service, Cart (with count), Account — cart is always one tap away on phones.

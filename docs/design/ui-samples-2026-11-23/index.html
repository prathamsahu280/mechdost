MechDost UI refresh — samples

I audited the live site (phone and desktop), studied the patterns Indian shoppers already know from big shopping, service and auto-parts apps (vehicle-first search, location line, image-led cards, chip filters, sticky buy bars, cash on delivery up front), and redesigned the five key screens in MechDost's own theme: navy, orange, Manrope + DM Sans, light and dark, English and Hindi. Open each page; every phone frame scrolls, and the Light/Dark switch is bottom-right.

HomeLocation + vehicle first, voice search, 8 category tiles, products on screen one. English light, Hindi dark, desktop. ProductFull-width photo, pack cards, fit check, delivery tiles, sticky buy bar; plus the "price on request" version. WorkshopsMap first, "what does it need?" tiles with prices, workshop cards, one-screen booking with expected cost. Order trackingArrival in plain words, the delivery code big, a simple timeline, help tiles (WhatsApp, bill, get it fitted, order again). Cart & checkoutFree-delivery progress, savings line, one-screen checkout with cash on delivery first.

What the audit found (live site, 23 Nov)

  1. Phone home is ~12 screens long; several sections repeat the same promises.
  2. Phone shop shows no products on the first screen (headline + paragraph + 4-field form first), and the headline says "Across India".
  3. Product page: small photo, price hidden behind the tab bar, related items one per row, five different actions stacked, internal codes shown as "Part reference".
  4. Emoji used as icons in the header, tab bar and announcement strip; ALL-CAPS labels on most sections.
  5. Supplier ALL-CAPS product names ("KELVINN UPHOLSTERY CLEANER FOAM") shown as is.
  6. No visible place for location / PIN or the customer's vehicle, which are the two things that decide what can be bought.

Design principles used

  • Location and vehicle first — every screen knows "Raipur 492001" and "Activa 6G".
  • Products and prices on the first screen; words come after.
  • Big targets, chips over dropdowns, one main button per screen, pinned at the bottom on phones.
  • Trust cues where decisions happen: genuine seller, cash on delivery, estimate before work, WhatsApp help in Hindi.
  • Hindi as a first-class language (Noto Sans Devanagari, labels that fit).
  • Same brand: navy + orange, real line icons, no emoji, light and dark.
